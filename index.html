Frequently Asked Questions

What is SehatConnect?

SehatConnect helps users explore healthcare services and submit appointment requests.

How can I book an appointment?

Open Find Doctors or Book Appointment and complete the appointment form.

Can I order medicines?

You can submit a medicine request and delivery address. Order processing and delivery integration are not yet available.

Are the doctors real?

No. The doctor profiles are fictional sample content.

Where is my information saved?

Some account and appointment information is saved in this browser.
